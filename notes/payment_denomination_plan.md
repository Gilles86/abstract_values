# Cash denomination plan — remaining 16 subjects

Plan for paying the **remaining 16 study subjects** in cash (CHF). Rounds every
payout to the nearest **CHF 0.10** (no 5-rappen coins used).

## Assumptions

- 16 subjects, each completing the **full 2-session** experiment.
- Payment = **CHF 30/session show-up** (= 60) + **variable BDM earnings** (~CHF 92 mean).
- Projected per-subject payout ≈ **CHF 150** (observed range 145.80–156.90).
- Projected total for the 16 ≈ **CHF 2,444** → keep **~CHF 2,500** on hand.
- Single-session subjects (e.g. sub-17–22 returning for session 2) would be
  *additional* payments, not part of this 16.
